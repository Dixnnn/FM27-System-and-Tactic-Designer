Third-party components
======================

FM27 Tactic Builder is free software under the GNU General Public License v3
(LICENSE.txt). Its squad reader (the part that reads the player table inside
FM27), the setup program and their tests are based on FM View Exporter by
ElectroHugin, GPL-3.0-or-later:
https://github.com/ElectroHugin/FM-View-Exporter
Those files say so at the top and were changed for this project (names,
English texts, the tactic page, the squad view, in-game messages).

The role list (names, key and preferred attributes) was transcribed from FM27
by the FM Player Analyzer project: https://github.com/ElectroHugin/FM-Player-Analyzer

The full package also contains an unchanged build of BepInEx 6 (bleeding
edge, see packaging/bepinex.env in the repository). Source and licences:
