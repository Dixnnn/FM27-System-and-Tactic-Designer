# Changelog

What changed in each version of FM27 Tactic Builder. Download the newest setup from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/Dixnnn/FM27-System-and-Tactic-Designer/releases/latest); running it over an older version
updates it and keeps your settings.

## 1.0.0 (11 Nov 2026)

- **Copy the tactic as text** (Export menu): shapes, every player's spots and roles, the changed instructions and the set-piece
  takers, ready to paste into a forum post, Discord or your notes.
- The browser tab shows your club's name.
- **Esc** closes the Choose players, Formation and Idea panels and the menus.
- Changing **Team strength** on the report keeps you where you were in the report.
- Fix: changing Team strength no longer drops the shortlist comparison (Signings) and the progress history sent by the plugin.
- A clear message if the browser blocks the page's scripts.

## 0.9.0 (11 Nov 2026)

- New look, "Floodlights": a night match under the lights, with a scoreboard for the key settings, and a matching light
  "day match" theme. Fonts are built into the page, so it still works offline.
- The club crest: in the page, the browser tab, the report, the cheat sheet, the shared picture and the setup program.
- **Club colours**: your players wear your kit on the pitches, in saved reports and in the shared picture.
- Players walk out of the tunnel when a tactic opens; ticking the whole checklist stamps it **Locked in**; a studio pundit
  has one line about every tactic.

## 0.8.0 (11 Nov 2026)

- **Your idea**: describe the tactic you want in your own words ("I want my striker to score headers", "press high",
  "park the bus and counter") and choose how hard to push it, from Normal to Extreme.

## 0.7.0 (11 Nov 2026)

- **Formation**: choose the in- and out-of-possession shapes yourself.
- **Checklist** with tick boxes in "Enter it in FM27", **player cards**, and **unavailable** players.
- **Signings**: F8 on a shortlist or player search shows who would improve your team.
- **Match plan** for your next opponent, **progress** over time, a **picture to share**, a **one-page cheat sheet**, a filter
  for the role table and a **cup / rotation XI**.

## 0.6.0 (10 Nov 2026)

- Visual upgrade: clearer fit colours, richer pitch diagrams and a section bar that follows you down the report.

## 0.5.1 (10 Nov 2026)

- The plugin installs its own squad view, **FM27TFT**, so the squad screen shows every column the tool needs.

## 0.5.0 (10 Nov 2026)

- **The FM27 plugin**: press F8 on your squad screen and the tactic opens in your browser. Includes a setup program that
  installs, updates and uninstalls it.

## 0.1.0 to 0.4.0 (9 to 10 Nov 2026)

- The tactic builder itself: reads a squad export, finds the best shapes, eleven and roles, all team and player
  instructions with reasons, set pieces, depth chart, training and alternative tactics; a dark design; **Choose players**
  (must start, marquee player, future stars).
